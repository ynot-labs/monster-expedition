Uninstall / 卸载

  1. Disconnect Codex Link in the game settings.
  2. Quit the Pet from its contextual menu.
  3. Delete /Applications/Monster Expedition.app.
  4. Optionally delete ~/Library/Application Support/Monster Expedition/ to erase the local save and visual bridge.
  5. Remove the plugin in Codex.

没有账号或云端数据需要删除。
